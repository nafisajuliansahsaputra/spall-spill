bounds are 4, 32 and 3 RPCs respectively. Issue and bundle share the issue request

never application wall-clock, to check/reset fixed-window action and work counts.
Only if both have capacity increment both; denial increments neither. Missing keys
initialize, but malformed state, backward server time or unexpected response deny.

or raw identity in keys, script arguments or logs. Capacity ranges are bounded;
configuration must permit each selected operation's cost, no silent capacity boost.

claim. Production policy/capacity/abuse evidence must account for that property.

1 only; errors, abort, timeout, noncanonical response and unavailable identity deny.
No retries. A late EVAL may burn capacity, never restore or over-admit; after late
identity no new EVAL starts. Identity/origin gates remain distinct from authorization.
